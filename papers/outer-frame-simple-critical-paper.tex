\begin{abstract}
We strengthen the outer case of a fixed nine-point certificate for
simple critical zeros of the Riemann zeta function. A different
baseline-only dual representation pays 201 captured frames; 35
previous frame witnesses are retained. Exact difference-constraint
closure and seventeen closed cases pay the remaining five frames.
The original 2,399 ordered pair domains are all replayed at the new
uniform reward $805460/10^8$. The unchanged all-zero lossless
transport gives the asymptotic lower proportion
$66812491/99194540=67.3550086526939\ldots\%$ for both simple and
distinct critical zeros, relative to all nontrivial zeros counted
with multiplicity. The new mathematical certificate uses only
original proved point and convexity data. It is separate from the
completed Lean and independent-kernel verification of the preceding
bound; no new formal or website acceptance is claimed here.
\end{abstract}

\section{Statement and retained framework}
Let $N(T)$ count all nontrivial zeros of $\zeta$ in $(0,T]$ with
multiplicity, and let $N_s(T)$ count its simple zeros on
$\Re s=1/2$ in that interval. Let $N_*(T)$ count distinct critical
zeros. Put
\[
 p=\frac{66812491}{99194540},\qquad c=\frac{805460}{10^8}.
\]
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
In the unconditional correlation and continuous-certificate
framework retained in \cite{Lossless,Ninth,Joint},
\[
 \liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_s(2T)-N_s(T)}{N(2T)-N(T)}\ge p,
 \qquad
 \liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_s(T)}{N(T)}\ge p.
\]
The same statements hold with $N_s$ replaced by $N_*$. Equivalently,
each ratio is eventually at least $p-\varepsilon$ for every
$\varepsilon>0$.
\end{theorem}
The denominators retain all line-exterior zeros and every
multiplicity. These are asymptotic statements, not a fixed-height
numerical count. Relative to the preceding bound
$p_0=66812491/99194552$, the gain in percentage points is exactly
\[
 100(p-p_0)=\frac{1002187365}{122994474451826}
 =0.00000814823080034\ldots.
\]
The purpose is a complete net improvement within a fixed
certificate, rather than a new correlation theorem.

We retain the fixed positive normalized AM density and its real
Fourier transform $K$ from \cite{Joint}. Write $w(t)=K(t)^2$, so
$0\le w\le1$. With the unchanged nonnegative pair coefficients
$a_{ij}$ and gap pressures $b_r$ recorded there, set
\[
 F_8(h)=\sum_{r=0}^6b_rh_r+
 \sum_{0\le i<j\le7}a_{ij}w(h_i+\cdots+h_{j-1}),
 \qquad h_r\ge4/5.
\]
For eight such gaps define
\[
 F_9(g)=\tfrac12F_8(g_0,\ldots,g_6)
       +\tfrac12F_8(g_1,\ldots,g_7)
       +2w(g_0+\cdots+g_7).
\]
The unchanged strict strong capture at
$\tau=805803/10^8$ covers every frame with $F_8<\tau$ by 241
original cells and their reflections. Compatible ordered pairs
are covered by the retained 2,399 difference-constraint domains.
Thus no additional captured leaf or analytic table is assumed.

\section{Complete payment of the outer case}
Set $\ell=805117/10^8$. The new certificate gives the following
continuous implication, rather than just values at sampled gaps.
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:frame}
Every real frame with $h_r\ge4/5$ satisfies $F_8(h)\ge\ell$.
Every compatible pair of captured frames satisfies $F_9\ge M$, where
\[
 M=\frac{32992055894668884928258784247}
          {4096000000000000000000000000000}>c.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For the original 241 cells we use 201 distinct baseline-only dual
witnesses, 35 retained frame replacements from \cite{Joint}, and
five new closed covers described below. The first 201 witnesses
use exactly the original 33-variable rows, with no new anchor.
They are the frozen adapter data accompanying the completed
formalization \cite{Formal}; their real inequalities are now
recomputed at $\ell$. They are not the old YA multiplier vectors.

The five new labels are $17,69,74,131,183$. In the following table
$S_{ij}=h_i+\cdots+h_{j-1}$ and $SC=32768$. Every branch is closed;
successive endpoints coincide, and the first and last endpoints
are the exact whole-cell closure bounds. Hence their union covers
the complete cell, including the branch boundaries.

Each branch closes the actual eight-node difference constraints
again. Its seven gap variables and all 26 square variables retain
complete real boxes, including $0\le w\le1$. Added rows comprise
exact closure bounds and valid tangents from the original point
table and convexity regions. Seventeen nonempty cases use 387
additional rows, 529 positive dual multipliers, and 288 distinct
original point entries. They replace whole cells, not selected
neighborhoods of numerical minimizers.

All 253 payments, $201+35+17$, are evaluated by exact rational
arithmetic. Their minimum is attained at label 17, case index 2:
\[
 L=\frac{8244400676681470233488767397}
          {1024000000000000000000000000000},\qquad
 L-\ell=
 \frac{2596681470233488767397}
      {1024000000000000000000000000000}>0.
\]
The gap pressures and pair coefficients are reflection invariant,
so the same lower bound covers all 241 reflected cells. Outside
the strict capture, $F_8\ge\tau>\ell$. This proves the first claim.

For the second claim we rebuild all compatible domains and replay
all their real boxes and exact residuals. The retained family has
2,342 original pair witnesses, 47 earlier replacements with 84
closed cases, and ten replacements from \cite{Joint} with twenty
closed cases. None is replaced by a stored summary of its minimum.
Their exact minimum remains $M$, attained at $(406,70)$, and
\[
 M-c=\frac{414294668884928258784247}
             {4096000000000000000000000000000}>0.
\]
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:nine}
Every real separated nine-point frame satisfies $F_9(g)\ge c$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If both eight-point subframes lie in the strict low set, the
complete pair cover and Lemma~\ref{lem:frame} give $F_9\ge M>c$.
Otherwise one subframe has $F_8\ge\tau$ and the other has
$F_8\ge\ell$. Nonnegativity of the total-span square gives
\[
 F_9\ge\frac{\tau+\ell}{2}
       =\frac{805460}{10^8}=c.
\]
The last safe bound equals $c$; it is not asserted to be strictly
larger. The stored full-frame minimum $L$ additionally gives a
strict positive outer margin of $(L-\ell)/2$.
\end{proof}

\section{Validity and all-zero transport}
We recall why the finite witnesses imply real inequalities.
If $v\le w(q)$, $d_-\le w'(q)\le d_+$, and $w$ is convex on
$[l,u]$ containing both the anchor $q$ and the entire physical
span interval, then
\begin{align*}
 w(x)&\ge v+d_+(l-q)+d_-(x-l),\\
 w(x)&\ge v+d_-(u-q)+d_+(x-u).
\end{align*}
This includes the derivative-interval correction and remains valid
when closure puts the anchor outside the physical span interval.
Every newly used point pack and enclosing region is from the
original proved PTL/REG source. New derivative sampling or a
strong-convexity assumption is not used.

For a branch write its actual inequalities as $Ax\le b$ and
$L_i\le x_i\le U_i$, with objective numerator $q^Tx$. A rational
multiplier vector $\lambda\ge0$ gives, with $r=q+A^T\lambda$,
\[
 q^Tx\ge-\lambda^Tb+
 \sum_i r_i\begin{cases}L_i,&r_i\ge0,\\U_i,&r_i<0.\end{cases}
\]
Every signed residual is paid. Floating optimization only proposes
the witnesses; the implication, closure, region guards, complete
boxes and closed unions are checked with integers and fractions.

The lossless counting transport of \cite{Lossless,Ninth} applies
to Lemma~\ref{lem:nine} with unchanged gain $A=67216841/10^8$
and gap fee $B=404350/10^8$. Sliding retains pair budget at most
two, pair mass below sixteen, endpoint fee $8c$, a close-pair
reward at least $(51/550)^2>c$, and a continuous majorant of mass below
two. Its smoothing loss remains $32d\varepsilon$. The Hermitian
Gram argument includes reflected off-line pairs and all zero
multiplicities. Taking the height limit first and the smoothing
limit second gives
\[
 \frac{A-B}{1-c}=\frac{66812491}{99194540}=p.
\]
The same retained transport proves the dyadic and cumulative
versions. Simple-zero inclusion gives the distinct-zero version,
completing Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.

\section{Reproduction and proof status}
The additive public certificate and standard-library checker are
\begin{quote}\small
\path{output/am-outer-frame-certificate.json}\\
\path{scripts/am_outer_frame_certificate.py}.
\end{quote}
Run the checker from the repository root with \texttt{--check}.
It rebuilds all 253 original frame cases, verifies reflection
symmetry, regenerates compatible geometry, and replays all 2,399
ordered pair witnesses. The 201 baseline adapter is included in
the public JSON, so an external formal-repository checkout is not
needed. Optional \texttt{--source} verifies actual original point
and region identity. Optimized Python is explicitly rejected.

The preceding bound $p_0$ has a complete ordinary Lean proof and
an independent full-kernel replay of 84,362 declarations at the
frozen commit \cite{Formal}. This paper proves a further
mathematical improvement, with a separate exact finite check;
its new witnesses have not yet been transplanted into Lean.
The older formal records, submissions, papers, and certificates
are retained. Website resource compliance and acceptance remain
separate pending tasks.
