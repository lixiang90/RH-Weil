\begin{abstract}
The outer case of a nine-point certificate can be improved without
enlarging its strong low-frame capture. We strengthen ten of the 241
captured eight-point frames, using 35 additional occurrences of
32 points in the original certified kernel table. The other 231 frames
retain their original exact duals. This proves a larger global
eight-point floor, which raises the high--low average, while the
previous complete low--low certificate already pays the new target.
The resulting uniform nine-point reward is $8054119/10^9$. The unchanged
lossless all-zero transport gives
\[
 \liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_0^s(T)}{N(T)}
 \ge \frac{668124910}{991945881}=0.673549759918807505\ldots.
\]
The numerator counts simple critical-line zeros; the denominator
counts all nontrivial zeros with multiplicity. The new finite
implication is checked with exact rational arithmetic under the
retained continuous point, convexity and capture certificate.
The preceding bound has a complete ordinary Lean proof and independent
kernel replay. The additional low-frame strengthening has not yet
been ported to Lean or accepted by the record website.
\end{abstract}

\section{Fixed framework and statement}

Let $N(T)$ count all nontrivial zeros of $\zeta$ with imaginary part
in $(0,T]$, including multiplicity, and let $N_0^s(T)$ count the simple
zeros on $\Re s=1/2$ in that interval.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
Within the retained continuous AM certificate and proved lossless
all-zero analytic transport,
\[
 \liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_0^s(T)}{N(T)}
 \ge \frac{668124910}{991945881}.
\]
The new input is the exact finite low-frame implication in
Section~\ref{sec:finite}. No additional correlation conjecture,
zero-free region or arithmetic moment assumption is used.
\end{theorem}

The preceding bound was $941021/1397107=66812491/99194597$
\cite{Ninth}. The present gain is exactly
\[
 \frac{668124910}{991945881}-\frac{941021}{1397107}
 =\frac{83750869}{1385854533966267},
\]
or approximately $0.00000604326550495$ percentage points. This is a
comparison with the project's fixed preceding result. No exhaustive
global priority or official record acceptance is asserted.

Put $\beta=1/\sqrt2$, $Z=\sqrt2\sin\beta$ and $I=[-1/2,1/2]$.
With zero extension outside $I$, the unchanged positive normalized
density and its real even kernel are
\[
 f(u)=\frac{\cos(\sqrt2u)+\sum_{j=1}^{12}c_j\cos(2\pi ju)}{Z},
 \qquad K(t)=\int_I f(u)e^{-2\pi itu}\,du,\qquad w(t)=K(t)^2,
\]
where the integer numerators $10^9c_j$ are
\[
\begin{split}
(&12310798,-15041681,3867664,6489926,-992327,5580097,\\
 &-6846472,3781297,-5670353,3355089,-450523,-218483).
\end{split}
\]
In particular $0\le w(t)\le1$ on $\R$.
For seven real gaps $h_r\ge\theta=4/5$, define
\[
 F_8(h)=\sum_{r=0}^6b_rh_r+
 \sum_{0\le i<j\le7}a_{ij}w(h_i+\cdots+h_{j-1}),
\]
where $10^8b_r=(28898,57272,75526,80958,75526,57272,28898)$.
The successive rows of $10^8a_{ij}$ are
\[
\begin{array}{l}
(13630830,0,19565904,20317441,45030671,100000000,200000000),\\
(29997996,30621878,47766489,79682558,109938656,100000000),\\
(37356140,69378121,65335210,79682558,45030671),\\
(38030065,69378121,47766489,20317441),\\
(37356140,30621878,19565904),\quad(29997996,0),\quad(13630830).
\end{array}
\]
There are 26 nonzero pair coefficients. Both the gap coefficients
and the pair coefficients are invariant under reflection.
For eight gaps $g_r\ge\theta$, put
\[
 F_9(g)=\tfrac12\{F_8(g_0,\ldots,g_6)+F_8(g_1,\ldots,g_7)\}
          +2w\!\left(\sum_{r=0}^7g_r\right).
\]

\section{The retained cover and the new outer case}

We retain the following proved continuous inputs \cite{PC8,Ninth,Formal}.
First, any frame with $F_8<\tau$, where $\tau=805803/10^8$,
belongs to one of 241 closed captured cells or its reflection.
Second, for all compatible pairs of captured cells, the full real
nine-point functional has the uniform lower bound
\[
 M=\frac{263917374155379049835090394947}
         {32768000000000000000000000000000}.
\]
The latter certificate has 2,399 physical pair domains and 84 closed
branches replacing the 47 weakest domains. It includes all finite-box
dual residuals. Its precise continuous implication is retained;
the stored minimum is rechecked from the existing rational witnesses.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:floor}
On every captured cell and every reflected captured cell,
\[
 F_8\ge \ell:=805021/10^8.
\]
Consequently this inequality holds for every real seven-gap frame
with gaps at least $4/5$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The finite assertion is proved in Section~\ref{sec:finite}.
Reflection preserves the complete functional, as well as the reflected
cell constraints. A frame outside the captured union has $F_8\ge\tau$
by the contrapositive of complete capture, and $\tau>\ell$.
The cover is continuous: integer endpoints encode rational inequalities;
the real gaps are not restricted to a lattice.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:local}
For every real eight-gap frame with $g_r\ge4/5$,
\[
 F_9(g)\ge c:=8054119/10^9.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If both eight-point subframes belong to the captured union, their
compatible pair is covered by the retained low--low certificate, and
$F_9\ge M>c$. Otherwise one subframe has $F_8\ge\tau$.
Lemma~\ref{lem:floor} bounds the other by $\ell$; the extra total-span
square is nonnegative. Hence
\[
 F_9\ge(\tau+\ell)/2=805412/10^8>c.
\]
This argument includes frames with equality at the capture threshold
and the shared endpoints of closed cells. It strengthens the actual
global eight-point floor, rather than extrapolating a finite low--low
margin past an unchanged outer-case bound.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact low-frame certificate}\label{sec:finite}

For each original captured cell we reconstruct the original YA lower
bound without rounding. Only the following ten zero-based labels have
this original lower bound below $\ell$:
\[
 17,53,64,74,111,131,148,166,183,213.
\]
The other 231 original cells already pay $\ell$ and retain their
original witnesses. The ten replacement problems have 33 variables:
seven real gaps and the 26 physical squares attached to nonzero pair
coefficients. Original span inequalities, gap boxes and all original
valid affine minorants are retained. Each physical square has its
complete interval $[0,1]$.

The additional affine rows use 35 occurrences of 32 original
PTL points, with scale $SC=32768$. The complete catalog records the
original point numerators, packs and PTL list indices. Four numerators,
$98272,230769,231200,265088$, are absent from the preceding formal
development's 1,089-point catalog; all 32 are entries of the original
2,196-point table. No new analytic kernel data are generated.
The source identity, point packs and convexity-region packs are
checked against the fixed original source \cite{PC8}.

For completeness, if a proved entry gives $v\le w(p)$ and
$d_-\le w'(p)\le d_+$, and the certified convexity interval contains
$[l_*,u_*]$ where $l_*:=\min(l,p)$ and $u_*:=\max(u,p)$, then for all
$x\in[l,u]$ the two valid affine bounds are
\[
 \begin{split}
 w(x)&\ge v+d_+(l_*-p)+d_-(x-l_*),\\
 w(x)&\ge v+d_-(u_*-p)+d_+(x-u_*).
 \end{split}
\]
They follow from the genuine convex tangent and the signs of the two
endpoint corrections. The checker includes both the query interval
and the point in the certified convexity interval. A point outside
the smaller query interval is used only with these valid reanchoring
corrections.

Write the reconstructed finite constraints as $Ax\le b$,
$L\le x\le U$, and the numerator of $10^8 F_8$ as $q^Tx$.
For each exact nonnegative rational multiplier vector $\lambda$, set
$r=q+A^T\lambda$. Then
\[
 F_8\ge\frac{-\lambda^Tb+
     \sum_i r_i\bigl(L_i\ \text{if }r_i\ge0;
                          \ U_i\ \text{otherwise}\bigr)}{10^8}.
\]
Every nonzero residual is retained, regardless of size. Across all
ten patches the positive multiplier support has 287 entries. Some
patches need only a better dual of the original rows; the total number
of added tangent rows is 35.

The smallest strengthened bound is checked exactly from all ten
patches and 231 retained original witnesses, and is at least $\ell$.
Hence $(\tau+\ell)/2>M>c$, where
\[
 M-c=
 \frac{2763379049835090394947}
      {32768000000000000000000000000000}>0.
\]
The same finite target is therefore paid by both parts of the cover.
A floating linear program proposes multipliers only; the accepted
implication uses the exact rational rows, signs and complete boxes.

\section{All-zero counting and verification scope}

The unchanged sum of gap pressures is $B=404350/10^8$. Sliding the
nine-point frame preserves every index-span pair budget at most two,
the pair-mass bound below sixteen, and the endpoint cost $8c$.
The original near-pair reward exceeds $1/40>c$, and the original
band-limited majorant has mass below two. The smoothing loss remains
$32d\varepsilon$. All off-line reflected pairs and multiplicities
are retained in the Hermitian Gram argument \cite{Ninth,Lossless}.
First let $T\to\infty$ at fixed smoothing, then let the smoothing
parameter decrease to zero. The proved transport and the unchanged
energy bound give
\[
 \liminf\frac{N_0^s(T)}{N(T)}
 \ge\frac{2-C_{AM}-B}{1-c}
 >\frac{668124910}{991945881}.
\]
This proves Theorem~\ref{thm:main}. Simple-zero inclusion also gives
the same weaker lower bound for distinct critical-line zeros.

The public checker and frozen data are stored in the mathematical
repository. Its ordinary check requires only the Python standard
library and the existing public rational helpers. With the original
source available, an optional source check matches all 32 entries
and their original list indices. The prior complete nine-point
certificate is reused; no new search of its 2,399 domains is needed.
Optimized Python is rejected because assertions are essential.

The complete ordinary Lean theorem and independent NaNoDa replay of
the preceding $941021/1397107$ bound are available at fixed commit
\texttt{c705c4a} \cite{Formal}.
They retain the previous proofs, submissions and verification records.
This new low-frame arithmetic implication has not yet been ported
to that formalization. Exact rational replay, full formal proof,
website resource compliance and official acceptance are separate
verification claims. No new zero-free region is asserted here.
