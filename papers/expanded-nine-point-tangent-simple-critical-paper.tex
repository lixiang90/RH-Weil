\begin{abstract}
We strengthen a joint nine-point certificate for a fixed perturbed
Montgomery--Taylor window. An expanded capture retains 241 low-reward
cells of an admitted eight-point certificate. Their reflections have
2,399 feasible ordered intersections. Existing certified point values,
derivative intervals and convex regions supply additional tangent cuts,
without a new kernel table. A standard-library rational verifier checks
every intersection with complete finite-box residual corrections.
The uniform nine-point reward increases to $805260/10^8$. The lossless
separation and all-zero spectral transport then give
\[
 \liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_0^s(T)}{N(T)}
 \ge\frac{66812491}{99194740}=0.6735487284910470\ldots.
\]
Here $N$ counts all nontrivial zeros with multiplicity and $N_0^s$
counts simple zeros on the critical line. The proof assumes no RH,
zero-free strip or higher correlation conjecture. It retains the
previously disclosed PC8 continuous and compiled-computation admission;
it is not a complete Lean kernel proof. This short paper records the
new certificate and its exact transport, with the analytic framework
and original data credited to their prior sources.
\end{abstract}

\section{Statement and provenance}

Let $N(T)$ count the nontrivial zeros of $\zeta$ with imaginary part in
$(0,T]$, including multiplicity. Let $N_0^s(T)$ count those zeros that
are simple and have real part $1/2$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
With the admitted continuous PC8, point-table and convexity inputs
specified below, and the complete exact finite certificate of
Section~\ref{sec:finite},
\[
 \liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_0^s(T)}{N(T)}\ge\frac{66812491}{99194740}.
\]
There is no additional unproved analytic hypothesis.
\end{theorem}

The original window and eight-point weights come from the fixed public
AM submission \cite{PC8}. The separation mechanism is adapted from
Knausg{\aa}rd \cite[Section~5]{Knaus}. Our preceding nine-point paper
\cite{Joint} obtained $66812491/99194897$. The present improvement
uses more of the already admitted point data and recaptures a wider
low set before proving a larger uniform reward. The exact gains are
\begin{align*}
\frac{66812491}{99194740}-\frac{66812491}{99194897}
 &=\frac{10489561087}{9839612017241780},\\
\frac{66812491}{99194740}-\frac{1669159}{2478195}
 &=\frac{517622017}{49164781738860}.
\end{align*}
These are respectively about $0.0001066054$ and $0.0010528309$
percentage points. The second is a comparison with a fixed arXiv v1,
not an exhaustive global priority claim. Neither zero-free boundaries
nor the original arithmetic fourth-moment estimates are improved here.

\section{Additional interval-valid tangents}\label{sec:cuts}

Let $SC=32768$ and $E=10^9$. A certified point $p$ with pack
\[
 V+2^{32}P+2^{64}M
\]
provides, for $q=p/SC$,
\[
 v=V/10^{10}\le w(q),\qquad
 d_-=(P-2E)/E\le w'(q)\le d_+=(2E-M)/E.
\]
Point values and derivatives alone do not establish an interval tangent.
The following direct branch of the original convex-region test does.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:extra}
For a leaf term with actual integer interval $L\le U$, put
$L^*=\min(L,p)$, $U^*=\max(U,p)$ and $k=(p+16384)\mathbin{\gg}15$.
Let $A_k,B_k$ be its admitted \texttt{REG} endpoints. If
\[
 k<16,\quad A_k\le L^*\le p\le U^*\le B_k,\qquad P,M\ge1,
\]
then $w(x)\ge v+w'(q)(x-q)$ for every
$x\in[L^*/SC,U^*/SC]$. In particular it holds on the actual leaf interval.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The admitted region makes $w$ convex on $[A_k/SC,B_k/SC]$.
The supporting tangent at $q$ lies below $w$ there, and replacing
$w(q)$ by its lower bound $v$ preserves the inequality. In the
original \texttt{tcheckPZ}, all containment and positive derivative-pack
tests pass, and both extension disjunctions pass by containment alone.
Thus its $n=1$ branch also gives the same full-interval assertion.
The case $L^*=U^*=p$ passes its singleton branch and is covered as well.
Each pack is taken from its original list \texttt{PTL\_i} with its own
\texttt{PTF} assertion; no new merged-table assertion is presumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:anchor}
For an interval-valid point tangent on $[l,u]$ containing $q$,
both functions
\[
 v+d_+(l-q)+d_-(x-l),\qquad
 v+d_-(u-q)+d_+(x-u)
\]
lie below $w(x)$ on $[l,u]$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $d=w'(q)$. The tangent minus the first line is
$(d-d_-)(x-l)+(d_+-d)(q-l)\ge0$; minus the second it is
$(d_+-d)(u-x)+(d-d_-)(u-q)\ge0$.
\end{proof}

Every admitted extra point is tested separately by Lemma~\ref{lem:extra}.
All its lines can then be imposed on the same actual square $w(x)$.
The old minorant's choice of at most two mixture points is no restriction
on this use. For zero-width leaf spans the actual $U=L$ is retained;
the enlarged interval used for a constant value lookup is not a tangent
domain. We do not take an invalid maximum of the two raw derivative
endpoint tangents across $q$.

\section{Expanded cover and exact certificate}\label{sec:finite}

For $g\in[4/5,\infty)^8$, set
\[
 F_9(g)=\tfrac12\{F_8(g_0,\ldots,g_6)+F_8(g_1,\ldots,g_7)\}
           +2w(g_0+\cdots+g_7).
\]
We prove the uniform reward $c_*=805260/10^8$, an increment
$c_*-c_0=2.57\cdot10^{-6}$. The nine-point pair weights are the
half-sum of the old frame weights plus two on span eight. All index-span
capacities are at most two; the total gap pressure remains $B$.

Use the larger capture threshold $c_L=805803/10^8$.
If either old frame has $F_8\ge c_L$, then
\[
 F_9\ge(c_L+c_0)/2=805403/10^8>c_*.
\]
It remains to cover the two-frame low case. The original clear COV
domains pay $c_0+(4/5)B$, and the large-gap domains pay
$c_0+(4/5)(B-\max b_r)$; both extra fees exceed
$c_L-c_0=8\cdot10^{-6}$. The small-gap domain is absent on
$[4/5,\infty)^7$. Hence the original 32 root trees and their bad
intervals suffice.

The capture preserves the original reward comparisons, return values,
validity guards and bit cursors. Only the diagnostic clone of the final
reward comparison changes to $805803$; its reward search uses fuel ten.
All 41 outer/root checks pass. A leaf whose stronger comparison passes
excludes the low point; a successful old leaf whose stronger comparison
fails is recorded. The initial capture handles the half-space $h_6\ge h_0$.
Direction leaves with $u_6<l_0$ cannot contain a point in that half-space;
empty-cell leaves contain no feasible point. This yields 241 original
cells. Their complete reflections restore the other half-space, giving
482 labels in total. Reflections send spans $(i,j)$ to $(7-j,7-i)$.
The cover is an outer cover; its cells need not consist only of low points.

The closed gap and span constraints use exact units $5SC$ after adding
the separation condition. The $482^2=232324$ ordered pairs reduce to
4796 after shared gaps, 2405 after shared spans and 2399 after complete
difference-constraint closure. Negative cycles reject empty domains;
shortest-path potentials give feasible real prefix coordinates otherwise.
No continuous gap is rounded to an integer grid.

In every retained domain use eight gaps and 34 shared physical
distance-square variables, so the objective has 42 variables. All original
enabled cuts, constant bounds and individually certified extra cuts are
put on the same square variable. The two-frame objective vector $q$
represents $2\cdot10^8F_9$. Write the constraints $Ax\le b$ and the
full variable box $\ell\le x\le u$. Squares have box $[0,1]$.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:dual}
For rational $\lambda\ge0$ and $r=q+A^t\lambda$,
\[
 q^tx\ge-\lambda^tb+
       \sum_i r_i\begin{cases}\ell_i,&r_i\ge0,\\u_i,&r_i<0.\end{cases}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use $q^tx=r^tx-\lambda^tAx\ge r^tx-\lambda^tb$ and minimize
each residual coefficient on its complete box.
\end{proof}

The certificate contains all 2399 rational duals. Only extra points
consumed by positive multipliers are retained: 924 point occurrences
from 237 distinct original points. Removing zero-multiplier rows and
remapping indices changes no lower bound. The smallest exact lower is
\[
 \frac{26386790900531855994044477203}
      {3276800000000000000000000000000}.
\]
Its margin over $c_*$ is
\[
 \frac{31220531855994044477203}
      {3276800000000000000000000000000}>0.
\]
Numerical optimization only locates multipliers. The final verifier uses
integers and \texttt{Fraction}, reconstructs all domains and rows, and
pays every residual by Lemma~\ref{lem:dual}. No floating-point
stationarity, optimality or feasibility assertion is needed.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:local}
Under the admitted continuous inputs, $F_9(g)\ge c_*$ on
the entire unbounded domain $[4/5,\infty)^8$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Outside the two-frame low case the stronger-frame bound pays $c_*$.
Inside it the complete reflected cover places the actual configuration
in one of the 2399 domains. Its real gaps and shared squares satisfy
the associated box and every valid cut. Its rational dual pays $c_*$.
All bounds are closed, so boundaries are included.
\end{proof}

\section{Transport to the actual zero count}

We give the parameter substitution in the already proved lossless
assembly \cite{Joint,Lossless}. Summing Proposition~\ref{prop:local}
over all nine-point sliding frames of $m$ separated real points yields
\[
 \sum_{i\ne j}K(y_i-y_j)^2\ge c_*(m-8)-B\operatorname{span}(y).
\]
Each pair is charged at most twice and each gap at most $B$.
For $m\le8$ the right side is nonpositive.

The same band-limited majorant is
\[
 g(u)=\frac{61}{100}
 \{\sinc(\tfrac45(u-1/2))+\sinc(\tfrac45(u+1/2))\}^2.
\]
The continuous certificate of \cite{Lossless,Joint} proves $g\ge f$,
$\widehat g(t)=0$ for $|t|\ge4/5$, and $\int g\le61/32<2$.
With fixed smoothing $f_\epsilon=f\chi_\epsilon^2/m_\epsilon$,
$m_\epsilon\to1$, let $d_\epsilon=\|f_\epsilon-f\|_1\to0$.
For $m_\epsilon>61/64$ the Gram matrix of the separated set has
norm below two, so the spectral clipping at level two causes no loss.
The total local pair weight is less than sixteen; each squared overlap
changes by at most $2d_\epsilon$. The sliding error is therefore at
most $32d_\epsilon m$.

Take a maximal disjoint collection of pairs at distance less than $4/5$
among the $n$ simple critical-line points. The remaining set is separated.
For each close pair the same window satisfies $K(t)>134/825$, hence
$K(t)^2>1/40>c_*$. Its two-by-two Gram spectrum lies in $[0,2]$,
and its reward pays $2c_*$ with smoothing error at most $4d_\epsilon$.
Convex spectral-trace pinching combines the pairs and the separated set.
For $\varphi_2(t)=t^2-(t-2)_+^2$ and their full Gram matrix $U$,
\[
 J(U):=\tr\varphi_2(U)-n
 \ge c_*n-B\operatorname{span}(y)-8c_*-32d_\epsilon n.
\]
This includes empty or short separated sets and every close pair.

The all-zero Hermitian operator $A$ retains all off-line reflection
pairs and multiplicities. Its remainder $Q$ after the $n$ simple
critical-line columns has positive inertia $p$ with $N-n\ge2p$.
The threshold trace lemma of \cite{Joint,Knaus} gives
\[
 \tr A^2\ge2(N-n)+\tr\varphi_2(U)=2N-n+J(U).
\]
The unconditional pair-correlation formula with fixed smoothing gives
$\tr A^2=(C(f_\epsilon)+o(1))N$ \cite{BGST,Lamzouri}.
The removal of its correlation weight and the order of limits are the
same as in \cite{Joint}: first $T\to\infty$ for fixed $\epsilon$,
then $\epsilon\to0$. The full height span is at most $N+o(N)$.
The bounded $8c_*$ edge term disappears, and the smoothing error tends
to zero. Consequently
\[
 \liminf\frac{n}{N}\ge\frac{2-C(f)-B}{1-c_*}.
\]
The same window-energy certificate gives
\[
 C(f)=\frac12+\beta\cot\beta+
 \frac{\sum_jc_j^2(1/2-1/(4\pi^2j^2))}{2\sin^2\beta},
 \qquad 2-C(f)>67216841/10^8.
\]
Thus the last lower bound exceeds
\[
 \frac{67216841-404350}{10^8-805260}
 =\frac{66812491}{99194740},
\]
proving Theorem~\ref{thm:main}. A fixed-width $7/8$ strip is not
used to turn off-line zeros into real Gram points or to reduce their
inertia budget.

\section{Reproduction and trust boundary}

The committed data are
\texttt{output/am-expanded-nine-point-certificate.json}; the standard
library checker is \texttt{scripts/am\_expanded\_nine\_point\_certificate.py}.
From the repository root run
\begin{verbatim}
python scripts/am_expanded_nine_point_certificate.py --check
python scripts/am_expanded_nine_point_certificate.py --replay
\end{verbatim}
The first command checks stored integers and all rational duals.
The second additionally compares the used packs and convex-region
endpoints with the fixed upstream source, regenerates an isolated Lean
capture with Lean 4.34.1, and compares every emitted field. The source
must be present at the path documented by the existing PC8 replay.
Neither command imports an upstream theorem as a new axiom.

The claimed continuous conclusion depends on the already disclosed
PC8 minorant, point-table and convexity admission, together with the
complete capture semantics. Integer runtime replay and standard-library
rational checks are distinct from Lean kernel proof checking. We do not
claim to have eliminated compiler/runtime trust or rebuilt all earlier
formal analytic inputs. The headline native-computation axiom of
\cite{Knaus} is not consumed. Independent full-domain reconstruction
and the build identities are recorded separately in the repository.
