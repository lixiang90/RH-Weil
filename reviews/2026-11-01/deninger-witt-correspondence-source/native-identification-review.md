## Typed native object

Write `A1` for the affine-line ring scheme over Spec Z and `A=O(X)` for the coefficient ring. For separated Noetherian schemes, the source defines

    Corr(X,Y) = c_equi(X × Y / X, 0).

These are universally integral relative cycles, proper/equidimensional of relative dimension zero over the source X. For normal Noetherian affine X, the relative-Cartier description applies to A1_X. This is an actual cycle group. It is not already a Chow group, Picard group, quotient by principal divisors, or Hilbert space. Work componentwise when X is not integral; the proof's fraction field K is for a normal domain component.

The product used in Theorem 5.1 is

    alpha star beta = m_* (p1^*alpha · p2^*beta),

with both factors on `X × A1 × A1` and target multiplication `m:A1×A1→A1`. Hence `Gamma_a star Gamma_b = Gamma_(ab)`. ## F/V, signs, and K-theoretic role
