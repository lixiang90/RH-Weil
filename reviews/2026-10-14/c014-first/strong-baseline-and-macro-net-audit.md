    integral_0^infinity [a-log(t/(2pi))]_+ dt = 2pi e^a.

    I(a)=4pi e^a+O(a+1), a->infinity.                 (4)

    B_old,opt(L,M)=1+(4e+o(1)) L e^M, M->infinity.    (5)

    D_L(L-r)<=S+w_n-kappa sqrt(n)(1-e^(-r/2)), 0<r<log2,
